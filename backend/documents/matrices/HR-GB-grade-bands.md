# HR-GB Grade Band Matrix

**Version 2025.1 | Effective 1 January 2025**

Grades 1 to 7, and which entitlements vary by grade.

**An employee's grade is held in the employee record and is
read from there.** It is never inferred from a job title, from
tenure, or from anything the employee says in their question.
A grade quoted by the asker is a claim, not a fact.

## G1 Band Structure

| Grade | Band | Typical roles |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Entry | Associate, Analyst |
| 3–4 | Professional | Senior Analyst, Specialist |
| 5–6 | Senior | Manager, Principal |
| 7 | Executive | Director and above |

## G2 Entitlements That Vary By Grade

The following vary by grade. Everything not listed here is the
same at every grade.

| Entitlement | Grades 1–4 | Grades 5–7 |
|---|---|---|
| Annual leave (see entity annex for the figure) | base | enhanced |
| Parental leave - primary carer | statutory | enhanced where the entity annex provides it |
| Parental leave - secondary carer | statutory | enhanced where the entity annex provides it |
| Notice period | per entity annex | per entity annex |

## G3 Reading This Matrix

This matrix says **which** entitlements vary by grade. It does
not hold the figures. The figures are in the entity annex,
because the same grade gets different numbers in the India and
UK entities.

Answering an entitlement question therefore needs both: the
grade, from the employee record, and the entity, also from the
employee record. Neither is in any document.

## G4 Salary Bands

Salary band data is **not** published in this matrix and is not
available to this system. A question about pay is referred to
an HR business partner.
