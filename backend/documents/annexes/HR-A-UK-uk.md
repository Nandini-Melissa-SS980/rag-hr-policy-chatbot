# HR-A-UK United Kingdom Entity Annex

**Version 2025.1 | Effective 1 January 2025 | Entity: UK**

This annex applies to employees of the UK entity. **Where this
annex and the global handbook conflict, this annex wins.**

## A1 Scope

Applies to all employees whose contracting entity is the UK
entity, regardless of where they physically work.

## A2.1 Annual Leave Entitlement - overrides HR-202 §2.1

Employees receive **twenty-eight days** of paid annual leave
per leave year, inclusive of the eight public holidays, which
meets the statutory minimum of 5.6 weeks.

Employees at grade 5 and above receive **thirty-three days**
inclusive of public holidays.

This replaces the twenty-five and thirty day figures in the
handbook, and note that the handbook's figures are stated
*in addition to* public holidays whereas these are inclusive
of them. Comparing the two numbers without reading that
qualifier gives the wrong answer in both directions.

## A3.1 Parental Leave - Primary Carer - overrides HR-202 §3.1

The primary carer is entitled to **thirty-nine weeks** of paid
leave: six weeks at ninety per cent of average weekly earnings,
then thirty-three weeks at the statutory rate or ninety per
cent of average weekly earnings, whichever is lower. A further
thirteen weeks may be taken unpaid.

Employees at grade 5 and above receive an enhanced **twenty-six
weeks at full pay**, offset against the statutory entitlement.

## A3.2 Parental Leave - Secondary Carer - overrides HR-202 §3.2

The secondary carer is entitled to **two weeks** of paid
statutory leave. Employees at grade 5 and above receive
**six weeks** at full pay.

## A4 Notice Period - overrides HR-201

Statutory minimum notice applies: one week for employees with
between one month and two years of service, and one additional
week per complete year thereafter to a maximum of twelve weeks.

## A5 Discretionary Matters

Shared parental leave arrangements, any request to carry more
than five days of leave across the leave year boundary, and
enhanced redundancy terms are **discretionary** and must be
referred to an HR business partner.
