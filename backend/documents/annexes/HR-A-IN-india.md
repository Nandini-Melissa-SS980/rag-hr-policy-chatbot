# HR-A-IN India Entity Annex

**Version 2025.1 | Effective 1 January 2025 | Entity: India**

This annex applies to employees of the India entity. **Where
this annex and the global handbook conflict, this annex
wins.** The handbook states the general case; statutory
minima and local practice differ here, and an answer that
quotes the handbook to an India employee is the wrong law even
when the handbook is quoted correctly.

## A1 Scope

Applies to all employees whose contracting entity is the India
entity, regardless of where they physically work.

## A2.1 Annual Leave Entitlement - overrides HR-202 §2.1

Employees receive **eighteen days** of earned leave per leave
year, plus twelve days of casual and sick leave, in addition to
public holidays notified by the entity.

Employees at grade 5 and above receive **twenty-two days** of
earned leave.

This replaces the twenty-five and thirty day figures in the
handbook, which do not apply to this entity.

## A3.1 Parental Leave - Primary Carer - overrides HR-202 §3.1

The primary carer is entitled to **twenty-six weeks** of paid
leave, as required by the Maternity Benefit Act, for the first
two children. For a third or subsequent child the entitlement
is **twelve weeks**.

## A3.2 Parental Leave - Secondary Carer - overrides HR-202 §3.2

The secondary carer is entitled to **two weeks** of paid leave.

The handbook's six-week figure does not apply to this entity.

## A4 Notice Period - overrides HR-201

The notice period is **thirty days** for employees who have
completed probation, and **fifteen days** during probation.

## A5 Discretionary Matters

Extensions to parental leave beyond the entitlements above,
leave encashment on exit, and any sabbatical request are
**discretionary** and must be referred to an HR business
partner. They are not determined by this annex.
