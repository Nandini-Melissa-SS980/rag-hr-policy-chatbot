# HR-202 Annual and Parental Leave Policy (2024 edition)

**Version 2024.1 | Effective 1 January 2024 | Superseded 1 January 2025**

This edition applied to leave taken between 1 January 2024 and
31 December 2024. It is retained because employees legitimately
ask what they were entitled to in a past year, and because a
grievance about a 2024 decision is judged against the policy in
force at the time, not the current one.

## 2.1 Annual Leave Entitlement

Full-time employees receive twenty-five days of paid annual
leave per leave year, in addition to public holidays.

Employees at grade 5 and above receive twenty-eight days.

Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement based on
their contracted hours.

## 2.2 Accrual

Leave accrues monthly at one twelfth of the annual entitlement.
New joiners accrue from their start date.

## 2.5 Carry Over

Up to five days of unused annual leave may be carried into the
next leave year and must be taken by the thirty-first of March.

## 3.1 Parental Leave - Primary Carer

The primary carer is entitled to **sixteen weeks** of paid
parental leave at full pay, followed by up to twenty weeks
unpaid.

Eligibility requires twenty-six weeks of continuous service at
the date the leave begins.

## 3.2 Parental Leave - Secondary Carer

The secondary carer is entitled to **two weeks** of paid leave
at full pay, to be taken within eight weeks of the birth or
placement.

## 3.3 Notice for Parental Leave

Employees must give at least fifteen weeks' notice before the
expected week of birth or placement.
